name: pitch-a-movie
description: Explains in That Movie Night Life whether a film is a good fit for tonight, for a particular group, or for a mood, using the app's spoiler-safe "Why People Like It" notes and content flags.
---
Use this skill when the person asks things like "is this a good date movie?", "why do people like The Thing?", "would my parents enjoy tonight's movie?", or "should we watch this or reroll?".

## Find the film

- "This", "this movie", or "it" while the person is looking at a film in the app refers to that film.
- No film named: use tonight's movie. If nothing is picked yet, offer to draw one.
- A named film: find it on the list. If several films match, ask which year they mean.

## Build the answer

Call the explain tool for the film. It returns the curated pitch when one exists, plus when it's a good pick and when to maybe skip it.

Then weigh the film's properties against what the person asked:

- The "good pick if" and "maybe skip if" notes are the strongest signal for fit questions. Quote their substance, briefly.
- The buzz kills say whether a film is violent, serious (heavy or bleak), or stupid (dumb fun). Mention a buzz kill when it conflicts with the audience or mood the person described, for example violence with kids in the room.
- The Letterboxd rating says how well it's regarded. Don't treat a lower rating as disqualifying for fun or cult films.
- The genres and the tagline help when the film has no curated notes.

Answer in two or three sentences, and end with a clear lean: a good fit, a maybe, or probably reroll. If it's a poor fit, offer to draw again with a filter that addresses the reason, such as avoiding serious films.

## Spoilers

The app is spoiler-safe. Never describe twists, deaths, endings, or the identity of a villain or killer, even if asked what happens. Describe tone, premise, and why people enjoy it instead.

## Comparing films

To compare two films, explain each one, then recommend one for the stated mood or audience. If the person agrees, use the set tonight's movie tool to lock it in.
