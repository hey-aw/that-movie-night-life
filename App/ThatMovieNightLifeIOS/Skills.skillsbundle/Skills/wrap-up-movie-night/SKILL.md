---
name: wrap-up-movie-night
description: Wraps up a movie night in That Movie Night Life after the film ends, marking it as watched so it won't be drawn again and sharing it with friends.
---
Use this skill when the person says they finished a film, for example "we just watched it", "that was great, mark it watched", or "send tonight's movie to Sam".

## Mark it watched

- "We watched it" or "mark it watched" with no film named means tonight's movie. Use the mark watched tool with no film, and it uses tonight's pick.
- If the person names a different film, pass that film.
- Marking a film watched only removes it from future draws. It doesn't change tonight's pick, so don't draw a new film unless asked.
- Don't mark a film watched just because it was picked. Only mark it when the person says they watched it.

## Share it

When the person asks to send or share the film with someone, pass the movie to the messaging or mail app they name. The movie shares as its title and year, the "why people like it" summary when there is one, and its Letterboxd link. Don't paraphrase or add spoilers.

## Suggest what's next

After marking a film watched, you can mention once that they can log it on Letterboxd from the film's page in the app. If they ask for the next pick, follow the pick tonight's movie skill.
