Watched by cameron j. 07 Apr 2026

I know that you love me…

Sponsored by Sony’s Walkman.

Wow… what a weird, interesting, but also intense ass film. The buildup is rather slow, and you kind of forget it’s a horror movie until that crazy ass 3rd act hits, but let me tell you… that shit is crazy. The film also looks great, has an awesome synth score, and has a lot to say up under the surface. It has a nice vibe, is weird, and I just had a lot of fun watching it, so I think you will too. Check it out!

Watched by Mateo Benitez 04 Apr 2026

I thought this was going to be a euro trash film so I was shocked at how artsy it actually is. The Fan is meditative and patient with its descent into horror territory. For the first hour you might even think it’s a romance film. That is until the wonderfully macabre ending. Maybe I should watch more German Horror.

Watched this as a surprise movie and didn’t realize this was in my watchlist until the scene from the poster happened lol.
