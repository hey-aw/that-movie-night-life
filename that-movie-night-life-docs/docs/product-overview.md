# Product Overview

## One-line description

That Movie Night Life is a movie-night companion app for iPhone that helps friends decide what to watch, enjoy spoiler-safe companion features during the film, and carry the moment into reviews, lists, and recommendations afterward.

## Product role

This product is intentionally positioned as a **companion** to Letterboxd rather than a replacement.

- Letterboxd is the long-term home for film logging, ratings, reviews, lists, and film memory.
- That Movie Night Life is the short-term home for the ritual of the night itself.

## Product pillars

### 1. Movie-night decision engine
Help people go from indecision to a pick with a small, strong shortlist instead of endless browsing.

### 2. Spoiler-safe live companion
Support lightweight, useful curiosity during the movie without becoming a distraction.

### 3. Post-movie handoff
Make it easy to react, log, review, recommend, and queue up the next watch.

### 4. Social continuity
Use rooms, recommendations, and recurring rituals to turn a single night into a habit.

## Core user moments

### Before
Questions the product should answer:
- What should we watch tonight?
- What fits the people, mood, and time we have?
- What is available right now?
- What from our watchlists or followed lists is a good fit tonight?

### During
Questions the product should answer:
- Where have I seen this actor before?
- Where was this filmed?
- What should I know that will deepen the experience without spoiling the film?

### After
Questions the product should answer:
- What do I think about this while it is still fresh?
- Do I want to log or review it?
- What should I watch next?
- Who should I send this to?

## Target users

### Primary
Film-loving couples and friend groups who already have movie-night habits but want the process to be easier and more fun.

### Secondary
Solo film lovers who want a better system for watchlist picking, companion context, and post-movie reflection.

### Tertiary
Film clubs and recurring groups that want a shared way to pick films, capture reactions, and build continuity.

## Core objects

### User
The person using the app, with preferences, connected services, and saved defaults.

### Room
A group space for a recurring or one-off movie-night context.

### Movie Night
A single session from planning to post-movie reaction.

### Candidate
A movie under consideration.

### Companion Session
The during-movie context state.

### Reaction
The post-movie output: rating, notes, recommendation, review draft, or handoff.

## Key feature categories

### Selection modes
Examples:
- Watchlist Draw
- Shared Overlap
- Streaming Now
- Under 2 Hours
- Followed List
- Wild Card
- Director Run
- Franchise Next

### During-movie tools
Examples:
- cast lookup
- familiar roles / known-for cards
- filming locations
- context drawer
- quick notes
- pause-safe prompts

### Post-movie tools
Examples:
- quick stars
- one-line take
- draft review
- read reactions
- recommend to friend
- related films and lists

## Product boundaries

This product should not try to become:
- a full social review network
- a replacement for Letterboxd
- a movie database first
- a generalized streaming app

It should remain focused on:
- choosing
- watching
- reacting
- sharing

## Success criteria

The product is successful if users say:
- it helped us decide faster
- it made movie night feel like an event
- it gave me useful companion info without distracting me
- it made me more likely to react, review, or recommend the movie after
